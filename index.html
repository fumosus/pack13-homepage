Welcome

A pack where kids grow through doing

Cub Scouting is hands-on adventure: campfires and hikes, STEM experiments, service projects, and games that build confidence. Parents and guardians are part of the fun — we do this with our families, not instead of them.

Pack 13 has been part of Plainfield since 1982. We’re chartered by The Plainfield Riverfront Foundation, and many of our families come from public and private schools in District 202. Whether you’re new to scouting or transferring from another pack, we’d love to meet you.

  • Character & citizenship through real experiences
  • Dens and committee led by parent volunteers
  • Year-round fun: clubs, campouts, service, and pack events

The Cub Scout Promise

I, (name), promise to do my best
To do my duty to God and my country,
To help other people, and
To obey the Law of the Pack.

Scouting is about doing your best — not being perfect.

What we do

Program built for every age

Kids meet in small dens by grade, then gather as a full pack for big events, celebrations, and shared adventures.

Lion

Kindergarten

First adventures with a parent or guardian by their side — exploring, playing, and learning together.

Tiger

1st grade

Discover the outdoors, try new skills, and build friendships with adult partners along for the journey.

Wolf

2nd grade

More independence, more badges, and plenty of games, crafts, and outdoor know-how.

Bear

3rd grade

Deeper skills, teamwork, and service — plus unforgettable camp and pack adventures.

Webelos

4th grade

Bridge toward Scouts BSA with challenging projects, outdoor leadership, and adventure.

Arrow of Light

5th grade

The final Cub Scout rank — preparation, pride, and a path to the next chapter of scouting.

Get started

Ready to join Pack 13?

New families are welcome year-round. Here’s how most people get started. Annual cost is currently around $300 per scout and can change with committee and insurance updates. Popcorn, holiday greenery, and other fundraisers can help cover it.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Email us with your child’s grade and a good way to contact you. We’ll answer questions and invite you to a meeting.

  2. 2

    Visit a meeting

    Tour the pack, meet leaders and other parents, and see dens in action. Kids are encouraged to jump in and try activities.

  3. 3

    Register & suit up

    Complete the official Scouting America application, pay pack dues, and get a uniform when you’re ready. We’ll walk you through every step.

Ready to apply?

Use the official registration link, or email us first if you’d like to visit. Ask us about cost, schedule, or transferring from another pack.
